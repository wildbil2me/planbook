Planbook

A gradebook and classroom assistant for teachers. Attendance, grades, the students who need you, and the messages that follow — all kept on your own device.

Open Planbook →

What it does

  • Attendance at the start of every class, fast enough to mark while students are still walking in.
  • Grades by weighted category, set up separately for each class, because no two classes are graded the same way.
  • The students who need attention, in both directions — the ones whose grades are falling, who are missing work or missing class, and the ones who have turned a corner and deserve to hear about it.
  • The message that follows. Planbook drafts the note to a guardian, counselor or administrator and hands it to your own email to read, change and send.
  • Accommodations where the work happens. IEP and 504 details, medical needs and behavior plans sit on the roster, out of sight by default, with a presentation mode that hides them all at once when your screen is on the wall.

Planbook works alongside your school's student information system. It does not connect to it and does not replace it.

Where your students' information goes

  • No server of ours ever receives it. Planbook has no server and no database. Everything you enter stays in the browser on the device you entered it on.
  • No account is required. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • If you choose to turn on Google Drive sync, Drive holds only the file Planbook itself created. Sync exists to carry one file — your year's gradebook — between your own devices, so your laptop and your iPad show the same gradebook. The one permission it asks for is drive.file, which reaches files this app made and nothing else in your Drive. Planbook works fully without it. Not in the released app yet.

Installing it

Planbook runs in a web browser and needs no download from an app store. Installing it puts an icon on your home screen or desktop, opens it in its own window, and lets it work with no internet connection at all.

On an iPad or iPhone, install it before you enter real grades. Safari can clear a website's stored information after about a week of not being used. An installed app is exempt from that.

iPad or iPhone

  1. Open Planbook in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the new icon from then on, not from Safari.

Chrome or Edge, on a laptop or a Chromebook

  1. Open Planbook.
  2. Choose the install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar, or look for Install Planbook in the browser's menu. Browsers move that item between versions; the address-bar icon is the one that stays put.

Anything else

Some browsers cannot install web apps, and some school-managed devices have installing turned off. Planbook still works in an ordinary browser tab.

Whichever way you use it, download a backup regularly from the ⤓ button in the app. It is the copy that can bring a school year back.
