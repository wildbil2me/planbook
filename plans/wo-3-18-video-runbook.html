Blocker 1 — there is no "scope in use" to film

Google's demo-video requirement is not "show the consent screen." It is show how each sensitive scope's granted data is actually used functionally. WO-7.2 — upload, download, the rev/baseRev comparison, the keep-both conflict — is 🔒 GATED and unbuilt. src/auth.js exports no writer, and a grep for files.create across src/ returns nothing. The Drive panel says so on screen, in as many words: "Nothing is uploaded yet."

So the most a camera can capture today is a teacher granting a permission the app then does not exercise. That is the "filming a stub" objection this work order already recorded on 2026-08-20, arriving one level up — it was written about a missing sign-in, and the sign-in now exists while the use of it does not.

Blocker 2 — the deployed app has no sign-in to film

hostAllowsSignIn() at src/auth.js:209 returns true for localhost and 127.0.0.1 and nothing else. On planbook.hwgteach.com the Drive section is never drawn, Google's script is never fetched, and that is exactly what keeps privacy.html's "no third-party code of any kind" true word for word.

The console half is already paid — both origins registered 2026-08-21, confirmed 2026-08-24. What is left is the one function, and it belongs to WO-7.3, which depends on WO-3.18. The paperwork wants a recording of a screen the code will not draw until the paperwork clears.

Blocker 3 — the client ID cannot appear in the address bar, and Google asks for it there

Google's sensitive-scope page lists "your OAuth client ID visible in the browser address bar". Planbook uses the GIS token flow — initTokenClient(...) then requestAccessToken() with no ux_mode, which means a popup. A popup shows a truncated, non-editable origin bar: accounts.google.com and no query string. The client_id is in that URL and is not readable on camera.

This one has a clean answer rather than a blocker's answer — shot 03 films the client ID off the Cloud console instead and the narration ties the two together. Do not switch the app to a redirect flow to satisfy a video; the popup is load-bearing.

What I would do

Shoot it once, after WO-7.2 — and spend the next two days on the half with no camera in it

A demo video earns no partial credit. A rejection costs another 3–5 business day round trip, and the two commonest rejection reasons are "insufficient functionality demonstration relative to requested scopes" and "demo app differs from submitted application" — which are precisely blockers 1 and 2. Setup

Before you press record

The fictional class

This ends up on YouTube, and it is your own FERPA rule before it is Google's. Invent the roster — names, scores, an absence or two, one behaviour entry so the log has something in it. Give it enough life that the gradebook looks used: a reviewer who sees three students and one assignment reads it as a mock-up, and "demo app differs from submitted application" is a real rejection line.

Leave supports empty on every student. There is no reason to have IEP or medical fields on camera, and presentation mode is not a defence you want to be relying on in a take.

The environment

  • A clean browser profile signed into the demo Google account and nothing else — the account chooser in shot 06 shows every signed-in account.
  • Bookmarks bar hidden, other tabs closed, Windows Focus Assist on so no toast lands mid-take.
  • Browser windowed, not full-screen — the URL bar must be visible in every shot.
  • UI language English. Google names it explicitly, and it applies to the consent screen, which follows the account's locale rather than the app's.
  • Display scaling such that text is readable at 1080p. If it is not comfortable at 100% zoom on playback, it is not readable for a reviewer.
  • OBS: display capture, 1920×1080, 30 fps, mic track recording — narration is the cheapest thing you can do to help a reviewer, and Google asks for it by name.
  • node tools/serve-https.mjs running and the app reachable at https://localhost:8443. Accept the certificate warning before you start recording.

Fifteen shots, about four and a half minutes

Ways this gets bounced

Five, in the order they are likely

What comes backWhy, and the fix
Insufficient functionality demonstration Blocker 1. The video showed a grant and no use of what was granted. No wording fixes this — only WO-7.2 does.
Demo app differs from submitted application Blocker 2. The homepage field says planbook.hwgteach.com and the footage says localhost:8443. Shot 02 is the mitigation; narrating the difference out loud is the rest of it.
Inaccessible video link Private instead of Unlisted, or a link that only works while you are signed in.
App name mismatch The consent screen renders the console's branding name. If shot 07 shows anything other than Planbook, fix the console and reshoot — do not annotate over it.
Branding not verified yet Scope review does not start until branding review passes, and they are two separate submissions in the current console. Submit branding first, wait, then declare scopes.

And the one that costs months rather than days

Adding a scope resets everything. A spreadsheets scope to read Roll Call!'s sheets, or a mail scope instead of mailto:, re-opens verification and puts the warning back in front of a teacher. drive.file is sensitive, not restricted, so there is no CASA assessment and no third-party audit — days, not months, provided the list stays at one.
