Overdue tone audition

Each option has a 5-minute sound and a more urgent 10-minute one. “Current” is exactly what Planbook plays today. Listen on the iPad, across the room, with the noise on.

No sound on the iPad? Check that Silent Mode is off in Control Center and the volume is up. The noise is a rough stand-in for a room of talking students, to judge whether a soft option still carries.

Every option is built from plain oscillators, the same way the app builds its tone today, so whichever you pick can go straight into the app with no sound files. Loudness between options is matched by ear, not measured; the one you pick gets its level tuned on the iPad when it is built.
