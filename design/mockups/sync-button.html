Tap syncs. Label: “Synced with Google Drive at 9:41. Tap to sync now.” A clock time, not “2 min ago”: it stays true without a timer ticking in the header — the ruling the Drive panel’s “ends at 2:47” already took. Tap reconnects. Label: “Your Google sign-in has ended. Tap to reconnect.” Drawn only after the silent renewal has been tried and failed. On the iPad a tap is the only thing iPadOS lets open Google’s sign-in window, so this button is the reliable way back in rather than a shortcut to it. Inverted fill — the presentation-mode button’s “on” grammar — because this is the one state where nothing syncs until the teacher acts.

Questions this drawing asked — answered by the owner 2026-09-26

Variant A or B? A is the header’s existing grammar — every control up there is a bare icon. B says its state in words, and on a touch screen a title attribute is never seen. Decided: A.

Phone width. The header row measured ~8px of slack at 390px after WO-2.29 added the fourth button (src/shell.css, the coarse block). A fifth 44px control does not fit. Decided: at phone width there is no fifth button — the About button wears the sync badge and opens About at the Drive section. Not drawn here; WO-7.5 ruling 2.

Beside the year, or at the end of the row? Drawn next to the year button because sync is about the open year. Decided: at the end of the row, immediately before About — so every header above shows it in the wrong place. WO-7.5 ruling 3.

When does “up to date” go stale? The app cannot see the other device, so “Synced at 9:41” is only a statement about this one. Decided: when the last sync was on an earlier calendar day — amber and a dot, like A2, reading “Last synced yesterday at 3:12.” Not drawn here; WO-7.5 ruling 4.
