Closing in

Dates with a consequence, and how long is left.

The reserved slot is spent. src/home.css has held .class-card-signals open at min-height: 24px since WO-1.10 so that the first real datum would drop into space that was already there rather than reflowing the grid. This is that datum. Nothing about the card changes — .class-card, .class-card-state and the slot itself are worn exactly as shipped, and the only new class on that panel is .gl-sig.
And the subtitle has lost its second sentence. index.html's copy today ends "…as those parts of Planbook arrive — the space on it is already theirs", and that file's own comment calls it "the one thing on this screen written to be deleted". WO-6.4 is what deletes it. A drawing that quietly kept it would be describing a promise the page had just kept.
Drawn mid-morning, not at 7:40. The phase goal is the 7:40 reading, but at 7:40 all five cards say "Not taken yet" and the panel proves nothing. WO-6.4's acceptance asks for the row to be correct "against a day with a mix of taken, dropped, and untaken classes", so the mix is what is drawn: two taken, one untaken, one that didn't meet, and one half-taken carrying WO-2.10's amber.
The chips on a card report; they do not tap through. WO-6.4 says every item taps through, and "3 to grade" landing on the attendance grid is a half-answer. But src/home.js's card has been a single <button> since WO-1.13 and a control cannot nest inside a control — making the chips real would put them beside that button, which means .class-card becomes a column, which is src/home.css's change to make and not a mockup's. The proposal is that the card says how many and the panels below say who, each of those being a real launcher. It is a defensible reading of the work order and it is not the only one. The owner's call.
“Progress grades due” is on the page twice — once under Today and this week and once under Closing in — and WO-6.4 asks for both sections by name. Either the deadline section owns grades-due outright and the week list skips it, or the duplication is accepted as the price of two honest questions ("what is scheduled?" and "what is running out?"). Drawn duplicated, with the second row saying so, so the cost is visible rather than argued.
There is no IEP/504 review date on this page, and that is two work orders disagreeing. WO-6.1 asks for review dates "surfaced ahead of time, in presentation-mode-safe form" and WO-6.2 puts them on the calendar. WO-6.4's acceptance says "Nothing on the page displays supports data, in presentation mode or out of it" — and a review date is students[].supports.reviewDate. Read literally, the one deadline a teacher is legally obliged not to miss is the one deadline Closing in may not show her. Drawn literally, so the gap is visible. Settle it before WO-6.4 is cut — the safe resolution is probably that a date-and-a-name is not "displaying supports data", but that sentence has to be written down somewhere before an implementer decides it alone.
Five panels is a long page on a tablet. At 1024×704 the class grid and about one and a half panels are above the fold, which means "who needs you" — the reason the app exists — is two scrolls down at 7:40am. Three ways out, none drawn: reorder (but WO-6.4 fixes the order), collapse panels 3–5 to their counts with a tap to open, or put the whole page in two columns above 1024px. Worth reading on the real iPad against this drawing before WO-6.4 is cut.

The class grid survives a quiet day; the other four panels do not exist. Whether today's attendance is done is not "something pending" — it is the question the home screen was built to answer, and it has an answer on a quiet day too. The other four have nothing to say, and four panels each saying nothing is exactly the failure the acceptance line names.
"Nothing needs you" is a claim, so it carries its warrant. The four chips are what was checked, in the order the missing panels would have appeared. A bare reassurance on a teacher's busiest morning is a reassurance she will go behind and verify — at which point the page has cost her the time it was built to save.
Does the quiet day exist in the first six weeks? Signals need 4–6 weeks of real data before they fire at all (Phase 4's own note), so from Sep 2 until mid-October every day is a quiet day for panels 4 and 5, and this drawing is what the glance page looks like for the first six weeks of the term it ships into. Either that is fine — the class grid and the grading queue are real from day one — or the empty state needs a second voice for "not yet, rather than nothing". Worth deciding, because it is the state the owner will see most.
