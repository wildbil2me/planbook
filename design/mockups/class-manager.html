The manager stays a modal — that part is settled. It is opened in August, revisited a few times a year, and it is a task you finish and dismiss. What is not settled is how many doors it carries: WO-3.1 made its rows six controls wide, and the three heavy gradebook screens becoming views is what stops it going to nine.

Six controls, and the third row shows the failure. A realistic class name pushes the action group onto its own line, so the row is two lines tall and the buttons no longer sit beside the class they belong to. .class-row wraps by declaration, which is the right call — it is what stops the labels running out through their own borders — but wrapping is a symptom being handled gracefully, not the row fitting.
This is also the version that does not get worse. With the assignment list, the score grid and the student detail all being views reached from the class view, no further door is added here by Phase 3. Six is the ceiling, not a waypoint.

Five controls, and the long name now fits on one line. The cost is a tap: Terms and Categories both move one level down, behind a panel that offers the two of them plus the letter scale WO-3.2 is about to add. That panel is also where a fourth setup screen would go without touching this row again, which is the part that matters more than the tap.
The badge stays on the row, and that is not negotiable in either variant. WO-3.1 put weights 95% here precisely so an unfinished class is visible without opening five panels to find it. Folding the door must not fold the warning — a warning behind a door is a warning nobody reads.
The note earns its keep in B and does not in A. "4 terms · 4 categories" was rejected as furniture when the buttons said both words; with the buttons folded away it becomes the only thing telling you what is behind the door.
This is a real fork and it is the owner's. A costs a wrapped row on long class names; B costs one tap on every trip to Terms — which, in the last week of August, is five trips. plans/gradebook-surfaces.md deliberately declines to pick, and says the call should be made against the finished row rather than predicted. Nothing in Phase 3 is blocked on it.
