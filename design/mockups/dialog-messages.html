The question. WO-3.56’s iPad reading found the assignment editor’s needs a name refusal drawing under Done, where it is easy to miss. Each of these ten dialogs grew a work order at a time, so a message lands wherever its work order put it. Every dialog is drawn below as it ships at v191, at the width WO-1.77 ruled for its kind — modal-confirm 480, modal-form 640, modal-report 900, and the Grade sheet’s one override at 980 — and, beside it, as proposed under the rule.
The rule, the owner’s, 2026-10-12, at dispatch: option A. A dialog’s messages — refusals, warnings, notes — draw ABOVE its buttons, in one place, for every dialog in scope. First case: the editor’s needs a name refusal moves from under the buttons to just above them. Not “beside the field.” Every “proposed” frame on this page is drawn under it.
The scope, the owner’s, 2026-10-12, at dispatch: exactly these ten dialogs — assignmentModal, assignmentMoveModal, holdModal, assignmentCopyModal, assignmentDeleteModal, categoriesModal, gradingModeModal, categoryRemoveModal, letterScaleModal and gradesRecordModal. Nothing added. The Scores Keys dialog and the past-due banner stay out.
How to read it. A magenta dashed outline picks out a message: a sentence the code draws only when something is the case — a refusal (red), a warning (amber), a note. Its tag numbers it for the caption. Standing prose that is always there — the opening paragraph, the grey hints, a field’s caption — is the dialog’s own text, not a message, and is not outlined. That line is the drawing’s reading of “messages”, and the few places it is not obvious are amber questions below. Hatched boxes are content left out because it is not what the frame is about.
