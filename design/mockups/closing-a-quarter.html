The same class in every frame: English III (B), 25 students. Quarter 1 ended Saturday 31 October and Quarter 2 began Sunday 1 November, so since WO-2.52 every class screen opens on Quarter 2 by itself. Frames A and B are Monday 2 November, the first time the class is opened after Q1 ends. Frames C and D are a week later, after two Q1 grades were changed. Every name is invented. The app's header is drawn plainly, as in the earlier rounds; the Scores grid under each bar is left out, because nothing about it changes.
What is stored, and what is not. Record Q1 grades appends one entry: the class, the term, when, and each student's percentage and letter. A re-recording is a new entry, and the newest wins, like the contact log. Nothing in the app deletes one. Whether Q1 still matches is never stored. It is worked out each time a screen is drawn, by asking the grade engine for the live figures and comparing them with the newest entry, so no edit can forget to flag itself.

Why a bar and not only a notice. Nobody taps from Q1 to Q2: the app moves itself on 1 November. A notice tied to a tap would never reach the teacher who does nothing, who is the one it is for. So the bar is the standing reminder, and it shows on all five of this class's screens while Q1 is unrecorded. It stays until a recording matches, as the backup nag stays until a backup is taken. It has no ✕.
It sits where the backup nag sits, under the header, with the backup nag's amber and button, copied rather than shared. If both are showing, this one goes second.
Question 1 — when does the bar first appear? Drawn: the day after the quarter's end date. Earlier is possible (from the grades-due date, if one is on the calendar), but a bar during the last week of a quarter asks for a recording of grades that are still being entered.
On the quarter's last day, the owner, 2026-10-23. The bar starts on the end date itself, worded for it: “Quarter 1 ends today. Record its grades for English III (B) once they are keyed into Blackbaud.” From the next day it reads as drawn above.

Question 4 — when does this notice open by itself? Drawn: the first time a class screen opens after a recorded quarter has stopped matching, once per change, and whenever the teacher taps that quarter in the term strip. The bar (frame D) is always there besides. Or should the notice never open by itself, leaving the bar to do it?
Recommended and taken, the owner, 2026-10-23: the notice opens when you leave a closed quarter that no longer matches its record — tapping Quarter 2, another class, or All classes. That is the owner's own picture of it: you make the change, you move on, and it tells you. It never opens while you are working in Q1, so correcting three scores is not three interruptions. A change made without visiting Q1 — a weight changed from Q2, or a sync from the other device — opens it once, the next time the class is opened. Never under a projector. Dismissed, the bar stays.
Question 5 — does the dialog say what changed it? For example "Chen: Act 2 quiz 4 → 9". It is the most useful line for a Blackbaud grade-change form, and the most work: a score's history exists (WO-3.33), but a weight, scale or drop change has no per-student cause to show. Drawn: figures only.
Recommended and taken, the owner, 2026-10-23: show the cause when it is a score, because that is what a Blackbaud grade-change form asks for, and the app already knows it: every score cell keeps when it was written and what it was (at and was, WO-3.33), so Act 2 quiz: 4 → 9 is every cell written since the recording. When no score of the student's changed, one plain line says a class setting did — weights, the letter scale or drop lowest — because those keep no history to name. Booked as its own small work order after the rest, so the notice does not wait for it.

Recommended 2026-10-23, awaiting the owner's nod. Keep the bar on home and give each class a button that opens that class on the recorded quarter's Scores, with the Record dialog open, so one tap goes from home to the dialog. Three reasons. The card has no room: it holds two chips in a slot measured for two, and src/home.js records that a third means a second row on every card on the screen. The glance page's Closing in panel is the wrong home: it lists deadlines ahead, and this is about a quarter already over. Nothing on home would leave the teacher who opens only home blind to it until they open each class. Like the class screens' bar, it names classes and counts, never a student, so it stays under a projector.
