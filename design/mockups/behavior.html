Entries are never edited or deleted. If you get one wrong, write another — the log is what you wrote down at the time, and that is the whole reason it is worth anything in a meeting six weeks from now.

Two taps means the quick entries write a complete record on their own. Tap the door, tap Off task, and the entry exists — the subject is the chip's own words, the kind comes from the strip above, the time is now, and the sheet closes. The two fields under them are WO-4.4's "optional detail" and are genuinely optional: a teacher who never touches them still has a log. Write it down is for when she has used them.
Behavior and note are the record's two kinds, and the strip comes first for a reason. docs/data-model.md gives the entry a kind of behavior or note, and they are not the same act — one is about conduct and one is a reminder to yourself. It is chosen before the quick entries are read because the log is append-only: a wrong kind cannot be fixed later, only annotated.
Pre-written now, with the door left open — the owner's ruling, 2026-08-20. The six ship as a fixed list, which needs no schema at all: they write the subject the data model already documents, and a written entry is indistinguishable from a typed one the moment it lands. That is what leaves the back door open. A per-teacher list added later reads the same records, so it is a settings block and a picker and nothing else — no migration, no second shape of entry, and nothing in this work order to undo. The rule that keeps it that way: a chip writes a subject and never a code. The moment a chip writes an id the app has to know about, the custom list becomes a schema change instead of a preference.
Both directions belong here, and the middle ground goes fourth — the owner's ruling, 2026-08-20. Left the room is gone and Showing improvement takes position 4, which is the first slot after the three conduct entries and the one a thumb reaches without reading to the end. That answers the question two ways at once: the sheet is not where trouble goes, and the list does not read as three bad things with two nice ones appended.
