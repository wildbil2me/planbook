⚠ Planbook is not installed on this device.

iPad Safari erases the stored data of any site you have not opened in about a week. Everything Planbook keeps — your roster, your attendance, your grades — is in that storage, so one quiet week over a holiday can take a term of work with it. Apps added to the home screen are exempt from that, which is the whole reason to add it.

On an iPad: tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar — the square with an arrow coming out of the top — then choose Add to Home Screen and tap Add. Open Planbook from that icon from now on.
On a computer: use the install button at the right-hand end of the address bar, or the browser menu → Install Planbook.

About Planbook

Planbook is a gradebook and communication assistant. It tracks grades and attendance, surfaces the students who need attention in both directions — concern and praise — and drafts the outreach that follows.

Everything stays on this device. There is no account and no server: your data lives in this browser's own storage, and Google Drive sync is an opt-in extra you can ignore entirely.

This build

There is nowhere to enter anything yet. This build has the frame and the storage behind it — one document per school year, kept in this browser — but no classes, no roster, and no gradebook. Those come next, and the backup file that gets everything back out of the browser comes with them.

School year

Planbook keeps one document per school year — classes, roster, attendance, and grades together. Everything else in the app shows the year you have open here.
